About

Built on two decades inside the networks we now advise on.

42NAV is led by Sagiv Draznin, a telecom and cloud executive who has spent over twenty years building and operating the networks that carry the world's traffic — from carrier packet cores to hyperscale cloud platforms.

He currently serves as Head of Innovation and Automation at Nokia, and previously held senior roles including Head of Product for Azure for Operators at Microsoft, SVP of Global Delivery & Customer Success at Rakuten Symphony, and SVP of Operations & Architecture at Rakuten Mobile Japan — where he helped stand up one of the world's first fully cloud-native mobile networks.

Earlier, at Verizon, he co-designed the AWS Wavelength mobile edge compute solution with AWS, deployed across 150 edge locations. He holds 120 granted patents in mobility, cloud, and observability, and chaired the Core Network Project Group at Telecom Infra Project (TIP), where he designed the automation architecture for Magma.

42NAV brings that same depth to independent engagements — practical, hands-on, and grounded in what actually works in production.
